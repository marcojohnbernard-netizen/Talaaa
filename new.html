I like you

a little poem, just for you

I like you, yes, it’s true,

At first, I tried to hide it too.

But my eyes are always searching for you,

Knowing that I’m not ready for a relationship, but I will try.

I like you,

But you don’t have to feel the same.

Just stay beside me, laugh, be near,

And I’ll be happy you are here.

I like you,

And maybe time will change my view,

But for today, this much I know

Liking you is enough to show.

Thank you for reading this far.
